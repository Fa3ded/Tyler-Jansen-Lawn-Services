The "Push Mower" Advantage

Big landscaping companies show up with massive, 1,000-pound riding mowers that blast through your yard. They often leave deep tire ruts in wet grass, tear up tight corners, and risk hitting your fences.

I do things differently. I use a high-quality residential push mower. It takes me a little bit longer, but it's incredibly lightweight on your turf, won't leave ruts, and allows me to give a precise, careful cut around your landscaping.
